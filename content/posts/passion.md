A lot of people are confusing a lot with passion, without knowing what it is. I can tell for sure that your consumption
of passion would have mostly come under the myth. It happened to me and I slowly understood the real meaning of it.

Love at first sight. Most of us already know that's a pale blue lie. This romantic myth never worked for dating. And it won't help your career, either. But we still listen to help gurus that we should do something we love. Never take a job just for the money. Just unfollow them and their thoughts.
⁣

Here’s an idea which I have followed, it worked for me and I guess it worked for the majority of the people.
You might take a job for the money and then find the meaning later. The passion comes after the fact.

If you're under 25 and looking for perfect answers as to what do with your life⁣
⁣
- there aren't any⁣,
- pick ANYTHING, passion does not matter, work ethic does⁣,
- You're not missing out and social media has lied to you⁣,
- if nothing else, earn enough $$ to be independent⁣.
⁣

Your problem isn’t choosing, it’s fear of committing to the wrong thing. The reality, there are no perfect choices and
indecision is a path to depression.
⁣
You think passion is something everyone has, it is something everyone finds it out through the process.
⁣
The vast majority of people work for money and enjoy competency and being GOOD at something.
⁣
The number of people who truly and honestly feel spontaneous "passion" towards something and KNOW that is what they want to do,
they are few and they are the MINORITY.⁣
⁣

What do you do then?⁣
⁣
You do this: you find the intersection of the following ⁣
⁣
- Your Natural talents ⁣
⁣
- What you’re willing to work hard at ⁣
⁣
- A skill/skills that are useful and you can be paid for

# Closing Thoughts:

Overall,
- Have a Hunger for learning
- BE KIND to people

It’s easy to be passionate when you’re good at something. But without the willingness to work, passion is irrelevant.
