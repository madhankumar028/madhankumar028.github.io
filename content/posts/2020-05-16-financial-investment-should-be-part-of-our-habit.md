```
Making money feels good. And even if its not particularly 
meaningful, it relieves you of the stress of NOT having

Similar way when you start increasing your money, 
you have to build a habit of saving money.
```

Disclaimer: I am not a pro on financial savings, I don't have any knowledge to increase your savings with compound interest. But I can help you to make a wise decision when you about to spend money. This is only for financial savings not for investment.

If you are a guy who constantly waiting for your paycheck every month. This is gonna help you a lot. I have been in the same situation and I changed my perception about the finances which results positively for me.

## Start weigh your purchases

I used to spend money on purchasing clothes for me at least thrice a year, which results in the overflow of my closet. The moment which made me realize is, I started forgetting about the clothes which I have purchased already 😜. It has not improved me in any way. This is one example, and everybody will have their way of wasting money.

Realizing you can only do so much with the surplus cash you have on hand after rent and bills are paid helps to limit extra spending. Most of us can’t have it all, so have a conversation with yourself when it’s time to choose. Chances are you will end up saving money, as you get in the habit of not impulse buying just because you can.

## Time is money

After I got a job I saw price tags — for food, clothes, travel, and education — in a very different light. I thought about costs not just in terms of money but in terms of time. I had never appreciated this relationship until I first started working and began earning real money for doing real things.

Therefore the cost of something is monetary value and the monetary value can also be translated to time as it takes time to make the money regardless of the method of income. So use your time wisely.

## Closing thoughts:

I am constantly looking to gain knowledge on investing my hard-earned and saved money. So far I found that Bond and Gold are good options to invest, will write a blog on investment soon after gaining some decent knowledge on the investments.

You won’t save money overnight. Nor you can change habits in a week. However, if you work at it you’ll find that in a month you’ll some cash in your wallet and a stronger belief that you can control your financial future.
